One line, read straight off the map: an overnight forecast for
**Dribbleford**, a city that does not exist.

> Overnight in Dribbleford, temperatures fall to minus two, the river freezes solid enough to skate on, and the ducks have already filed complaints.

A hard freeze, a river you could skate on, and ducks who have taken
the matter up with the authorities — delivered with the calm confidence
of a meteorologist who has seen it all before and been impressed by none
of it.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the forecast to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$ENTRY_OUTPUT`
when set), after `validate()` checks it: a single line, 1–160 characters,
naming the invented city, reading as a real forecast, and carrying its
deadpan twist.

Run it:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
